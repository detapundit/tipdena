                🎯 THE MULTIBAGGER CHEAT SHEET 🎯
===================================================================

  PHASE 1: BUSINESS EFFICIENCY & GROWTH
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 ROCE (Return on Capital Employed)  -->  Target: > 15%
     [Why]: Shows how efficiently the engine churns profits from capital.
  
  🔹 Sales & Operating Margin (OPM)     -->  Target: Rising or Stable
     [Why]: Proves strong consumer demand and pricing power.


  PHASE 2: FINANCIAL HEALTH & SAFETY
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 Debt-to-Equity Ratio              -->  Target: < 0.5 (Ideal: 0)
     [Why]: Keeps the company completely immune to bankruptcy.
  
  🔹 Free Cash Flow (FCF)               -->  Target: ≥ Net Profit
     [Why]: Verifies that paper profits are turning into hard cash.


  PHASE 3: VALUATION (THE PRICE CHECK)
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 PEG Ratio                          -->  Target: < 1.0
     [Why]: Ensures you are not overpaying for the growth.
     Scale: <1.0 Deep Value  |  1.0 - 1.5 Fair  |  >1.5 Overvalued


  PHASE 4: CORPORATE GOVERNANCE & TRUST
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 Promoter Shareholding             -->  Target: > 50%
     [Why]: Ensures major skin in the game (Exception: Nifty giants).
  
  🔹 Pledged Shares Percentage          -->  Target: Absolute 0%
     [Why]: Zero risk of banks seizing and selling promoter shares.
