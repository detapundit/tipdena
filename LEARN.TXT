                🎯 THE MULTIBAGGER CHEAT SHEET 🎯

  PHASE 1: BUSINESS EFFICIENCY & GROWTH
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 ROCE (Return on Capital Employed)  -->  Target: > 15%
     [Why]: Shows how efficiently the engine churns profits from capital.
  
  🔹 Sales & Operating Margin (OPM)     -->  Target: Rising or Stable
     [Why]: Proves strong consumer demand and pricing power.


  PHASE 2: FINANCIAL HEALTH & SAFETY
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 Debt-to-Equity Ratio              -->  Target: < 0.5 (Ideal: 0)
     [Why]: Keeps the company completely immune to bankruptcy.
  
  🔹 Free Cash Flow (FCF)               -->  Target: ≥ Net Profit
     [Why]: Verifies that paper profits are turning into hard cash.


  PHASE 3: VALUATION (THE PRICE CHECK)
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 PEG Ratio                          -->  Target: < 1.0
     [Why]: Ensures you are not overpaying for the growth.
     Scale: <1.0 Deep Value  |  1.0 - 1.5 Fair  |  >1.5 Overvalued


  PHASE 4: CORPORATE GOVERNANCE & TRUST
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  🔹 Promoter Shareholding             -->  Target: > 50%
     [Why]: Ensures major skin in the game (Exception: Nifty giants).
  
  🔹 Pledged Shares Percentage          -->  Target: Absolute 0%
     [Why]: Zero risk of banks seizing and selling promoter shares.

================================================================================
                    🌟 THE MASTER INVESTOR'S EXECUTION ROADMAP 🌟
================================================================================

STEP 1: THE FUNDAMENTAL FILTER (Is the business a compounding engine?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] ROCE                              -->  Must be > 15% (Shows high efficiency)
[ ] Debt-to-Equity                    -->  Must be < 0.5 (Ideal: Near Zero Debt)
[ ] Sales & Operating Margins (OPM)   -->  Must be Steadily Rising or Stable
[ ] Free Cash Flow (FCF)              -->  Must be ≥ Net Profit (Over 5-10 yrs)
[ ] PEG Ratio                         -->  Must be < 1.0 (Value) or 1.0-1.5 (Fair)
[ ] Promoter Shareholding             -->  Must be > 50% (High skin in the game)
[ ] Pledged Shares                    -->  Must be Absolute 0% (No loan distress)

STEP 2: THE TECHNICAL ENTRY (Are you buying at a wholesale discount floor?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Horizontal Support Floor         -->  Price is sitting at a historical floor.
[ ] Dynamic 200 DMA Floor             -->  Price is touching the 10-month average.
[ ] Institutional Volume Footprint    -->  A tall green volume bar confirms a bounce.
[ ] THE CONFLUENCE ZONE               -->  All 3 of the above line up perfectly!
                  - OR -
[ ] The Breakout & Retest             -->  Price smashes a ceiling on massive volume, 
                                           and you buy when it retests as a new floor.

STEP 3: THE DEFENSIVE SHIELD (Is your capital 100% protected?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Portfolio Concentration           -->  Maintain a healthy basket of 15-20 stocks.
[ ] Individual Core Caps              -->  Max 5% to 8% allocation per safe leader.
[ ] Individual Satellite Caps         -->  Max 2% to 3% allocation per volatile small-cap.
[ ] Asset Rebalancing                 -->  Gently trim if a single stock crosses 25%.
